Project 1

In the first project, I had the opportunity to work closely with my team members as we constructed various logic gates together, which was both challenging and rewarding. We successfully built several types of gates, including XOR, NOR, AND, OR, and NOT, each requiring careful planning and collaboration. Throughout the process, we learned a lot about digital circuits and teamwork. To document our experience and share our knowledge, we recorded a detailed video of our project and posted it on YouTube for others to learn from and enjoy.
