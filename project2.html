Project 2

In the second project, my team and I visited a school that was facing difficult conditions, with limited resources and infrastructure, to teach and inspire the children there. Because the school did not have computers or internet access, we worked together to borrow as many laptops as we could from friends and family, and even shared our own internet connection to create a better learning environment. We aimed to make technology accessible and engaging for the students, encouraging them to ask questions and participate actively in the lessons. This experience was deeply meaningful, as we saw firsthand how our efforts could positively impact the children's interest in IT and their overall performance in class.
